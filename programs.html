A Progressive Learning Journey

The AISTEMiQ brochure outlines an age-wise progression from foundational STEM exploration to advanced technology.

Ages 8–10 STEM, circuits, sensors and science.
Ages 11–12 Electronics, sensors and automation.
Ages 13–14 Robotics, embedded systems and programming.
Ages 15–17 Python, AI, robotics, IoT and advanced technology.
