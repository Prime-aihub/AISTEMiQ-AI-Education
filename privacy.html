AISTEMiQ · WEBSITE POLICIES

Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the AISTEMiQ website handles information when you browse our pages or contact us about educational programs, curriculum, robotics kits and institutional partnerships.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Information You Choose to Provide

When you complete an enquiry form, you may enter your name, phone number, school or organisation, program of interest, student age group, estimated participant count and message. Please avoid including children's sensitive personal information in general enquiries.

2. How Enquiry Forms Work

The contact and program enquiry forms currently prepare a message and open WhatsApp using a link. The website code described by these forms does not itself submit that form to an AISTEMiQ website database. You choose whether to send the message in WhatsApp. Information you send may then be processed by WhatsApp and received by AISTEMiQ.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information you voluntarily send may be used to:

  • Respond to enquiries and requests for quotations.
  • Discuss educational programs, curriculum and lab requirements.
  • Communicate about a potential partnership or requested service.
  • Follow up on your enquiry where appropriate.

4. Hosting and Third-Party Services

This website may be hosted on a third-party platform such as GitHub Pages. Hosting providers may process technical information, such as server logs, under their own terms and privacy practices. When you follow an external link or use WhatsApp, that service's own privacy policy and terms apply.

5. Cookies and Analytics

The pages may use browser-side scripts for ordinary website functions. This policy does not claim that no technical logs or cookies are ever processed by hosting or third-party services. If analytics, advertising trackers or additional data-collection tools are added, this policy should be updated to describe them accurately.

6. Sharing of Information

AISTEMiQ should only use or share enquiry information as appropriate to respond to your request, provide a requested service, comply with applicable legal obligations or address legitimate security needs. This draft does not authorise the sale of personal information.

7. Data Security and Retention

Please share only the information needed to discuss your enquiry. Messages sent through WhatsApp may remain in your account and the recipient's account according to the applicable service settings. Any information retained separately by AISTEMiQ should be handled securely and kept only as long as reasonably necessary or legally required.

8. Children's Information

AISTEMiQ provides educational content for students. Parents, guardians and institutions should contact us on behalf of children where appropriate and avoid sending unnecessary personal details about individual students through general enquiry forms.

9. Your Requests and Questions

To ask about information you have sent to AISTEMiQ, or to request a correction or deletion where applicable, contact us using the details below. Requests concerning messages held by third-party platforms may also need to be made directly to those platforms.

WhatsApp: +91 90041 30508

10. Policy Updates

This policy may be revised when our website, services or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
